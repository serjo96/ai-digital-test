# Shelf Ready — implementation note

**Status.** The local MVP is technically complete through P0.3, with one human-signoff step still open. Accepted B1-v2 accounts for all 220 rows as 156 canonical products over 216 product rows plus four non-products. B3 generation and independent claim verification were run on the full input, replayed offline, and exposed in the same local viewer. Matching labels remain provisional until the reviewer confirms all 20 cases.

**Architecture.** A Node 24 / TypeScript Nest standalone CLI reads the original feed and closed taxonomy, keeps offers separate from canonical products, preserves exact source evidence, and stores immutable run reports and metrics. B3 generates only from allowed supports and fail-closed verifies complete atomic claims. React reads a validated snapshot of those artifacts; it does not call models or recompute matching/verifier decisions. Separate listing and matching-review screens export hash-bound human decisions.

**Three trade-offs.** (1) Conservative matching preserves variants and accessories; uncertain relations stay separate or in review. (2) B2 semantic extraction was measured but rejected, so the accepted product baseline remains deterministic B1 while B3 handles only publication wording. (3) Saved JSON/cache and an offline viewer make the result reproducible, at the cost of a deliberate manual signoff rather than an automatic approval workflow.

**Evidence and failures.** Full-input B3 produced 154/156 ready products, two identity-review products, no withheld products, and 390 structurally valid atomic claims. Its live run used 322 calls, 526449 tokens and $8.110955; one malformed verifier support ID was rejected and recovered by the single allowed repair. Offline replay reproduced the decision and publication hashes with zero calls. The first holdout replay also used zero calls: 6 cases/49 rows, TP/FP/FN 22/0/0, true negatives 1154/1154 and hard negatives 256/256. These matching figures remain explicitly provisional until human label review. Holdout is now revealed; any later tuning against it must be reported as post-holdout development.

**To finish.** The reviewer opens **Check product matching**, confirms or rejects the complete partition for all 20 cases, enters their name and exports `labels-human-verified.json`. After validating that export, rerun development and holdout offline and perform the clean-clone install/test/build/replay/UI check. No additional model run is required for this closure.
